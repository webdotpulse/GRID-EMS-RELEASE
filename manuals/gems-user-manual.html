1. Welcome to GEMS: Why Your Home Needs an Energy Brain

Welcome to GEMS (Grid Energy Management System) by New Energy Grid and The Charge Grid! Installed directly inside your electrical cabinet on an industrial Raspberry Pi 5 edge computer with ultra-durable NVMe solid-state storage, GEMS is the autonomous conductor of your entire electrical ecosystem.

☀️🔋
Maximize Free Solar & Storage
Every surplus watt from your Solis Hybrid Inverter is routed first to your household, then into your home battery, then into your Raedian EV Charger, and finally into hot water thermal storage—boosting self-consumption up to 85%+.
🛡️📉
Flanders Peak Shaving & Fuse Guard
Reads your digital P1 meter every second. If cooking and laundry spike your load, GEMS instantly discharges your battery and throttles your EV charger so your 15-minute Capaciteitstarief peak stays low and your main breaker never trips.
💶⚡
Dynamic Tariff & Negative Price Shield
Downloads Day-Ahead EPEX Spot prices at 13:00 CET daily. Charges your battery and EV during the cheapest night hours and protects you from paying export penalties when afternoon solar prices turn negative.
🔒 100% Local Edge Autonomy — Zero Subscriptions, Zero Cloud Lock-In

Unlike cloud-dependent gadgets that stop working when your internet drops or charge monthly fees, GEMS executes all measurement, dynamic load balancing, battery dispatching, and tariff formulas 100% locally inside your home. Optionally, you can pair with GRID-EMS-SERVER (https://ems.newenergygrid.com) for remote smartphone access and installer diagnostics—completely free.

6. EV Charging Modes, Automatic 1P/3P Switching & Split-Billing

Whether you tap the EV Charger circle on the Dashboard or configure your Raedian wallbox in Settings, GEMS gives you four intelligent charging modes plus automatic single-phase/three-phase solar adaptation:

🚗 When to Use Each EV Charging Mode
1. ECO (Smart Solar + Cheap Spot Top-Up)
Best for everyday use! Prioritizes free solar surplus during the day and automatically tops up during the configured Cheapest Spot Price Hours (e.g. 01:00–05:00) or when the Belgian Elia Green Grid Share exceeds 65%.
2. PV ONLY (100% Pure Sunshine)
Best for sunny days when working from home. Starts only when net solar export exceeds the 6A EV minimum (1.4 kW on 1P / 4.1 kW on 3P). Never pulls paid power from the grid!
3. FAST (Max Safe Speed)
Best when you need to leave soon. Charges immediately at maximum rated power (11 kW or 22 kW) while GEMS Dynamic Load Balancing still protects your main house fuse from overloading!
4. OFF (Paused / Hold)
Best when reserving battery power for home use. Keeps the charging session paused even while the cable remains plugged into the vehicle.
🔄 How Automatic 1P/3P Solar Phase Switching Works (1.4 kW → 22 kW Continuous Range)

Normally, a 3-phase EV charger requires at least 3 × 6A = 4.14 kW of solar surplus before it can start—wasting smaller morning/afternoon sunshine! When you enable Automatic 1P/3P Solar Phase Switching on a supported charger (such as the Raedian NEO), GEMS starts charging on 1-Phase (1.4 kW to 3.6 kW) as soon as 1.4 kW of solar is available. Once solar surplus stays above 4.2 kW, GEMS sends a pause command, waits a mandatory 20-second anti-welding safety delay so vehicle onboard relays open under zero load, switches the contactor to 3-Phase (4.1 kW to 22 kW), and resumes charging seamlessly!

8.2 Optimization Strategy, Flanders Peak Shaving & GSC Subsidy Protection

🧭 Which Optimization Mode Should You Choose?
1. Eco (Max Self-Consumption)
Choose when: You live in Wallonia, Brussels, or have a standard meter without 15-minute capacity billing. Maximizes solar usage inside the home.
2. Flanders (Peak Shaving)
Choose when: You live in Flanders (Fluvius). Activates the 15-minute rolling average Capaciteitstarief peak shaver and Smart Adaptive Ceiling!
3. Netherlands (Smart Saldering)
Choose when: You live in the Netherlands. Optimizes for Dutch EPEX Spot, Energiebelasting, and prevents costly negative-price feed-in fees (terugleverkosten).
Capacity Peak Limit (kW) Default: 2.50 kW
What It Does Your target ceiling for the Flanders 15-minute rolling average grid import (Fluvius bills a minimum baseline of 2.50 kW).
Smart Adaptive Ceiling If an unavoidable spike (e.g. 3.8 kW when the battery was empty) occurs mid-month, GEMS automatically raises its active ceiling to 3.8 kW for the rest of that month so you enjoy faster charging at zero extra grid cost, then resets to 2.50 kW on the 1st of the next month!
Peak Shaving Buffer (W) & Hysteresis (W) 150 W / 250 W
What It Does Peak Shaving Buffer (W) (default 150 W) triggers battery discharge slightly before you hit the exact limit (e.g. at 2,350 W) so sudden appliance spikes never overshoot. Ramp-up Hysteresis (W) (default 250 W) prevents EV chargers from rapidly oscillating up and down when clouds pass.
Active Inverter Curtailment & Base Threshold Toggle + 0.00 €/kWh
What It Does When enabled, GEMS dynamically throttles your Solis inverter down to match house load whenever your solar export price drops below the Base Export Price Threshold (€/kWh) (default 0.00 €/kWh) and your battery/EV are already full.
Green Power Certificates (GSC) Subsidy Presets: €0 to €450/MWh
What It Does Protects Flemish installations with legacy Groenestroomcertificaten (e.g., €90, €210, €250, €350, or €450 per 1,000 kWh).
Formula Effective Curtailment Trigger = Base Threshold - (GSC / 1000). With a €250 GSC, GEMS keeps your solar panels running at 100% until spot prices fall below -€0.250/kWh, so you never lose your green certificate payout!

Every Relay Automation Rule Setting Explained

Condition Type (4 Triggers) Solar or Spot Price
• solar_export_exceeds: Triggers when net solar export to the street exceeds X Watts (e.g. 1500 W).
• grid_import_below: Triggers when grid import is below X Watts.
• spot_price_below: Triggers when dynamic electricity price is ≤ X €/kWh (e.g. 0.04 €/kWh).
• spot_price_negative: Triggers automatically whenever wholesale prices drop below €0.00/kWh!
Min Run Time (sec) & Min Off Time (sec) Compressor Protection
What It Does Prevents heat pump compressors and contactors from rapidly clicking ON and OFF ("short-cycling") when passing clouds briefly dip solar production.
Recommended For heat pumps, set Minimum Run Time to 900–1800 sec (15–30 min) and Minimum Off Time to 600 sec (10 min). For resistive water boilers, 300 sec (5 min) is ideal.

SG-Ready Heat Pump 2-Relay Operating Matrix

SG-Ready State Relay 1 : Relay 2 Heat Pump Behavior & When GEMS Activates It
State 1 — Utility Lockout 1 : 0 Temporarily blocks compressor during extreme capacity peaks (if configured).
State 2 — Normal Eco Mode 0 : 0 Standard thermostat operation (default everyday state).
State 3 — Solar Boost Recommendation 0 : 1 Raises domestic hot water & buffer tank temperature target when moderate solar surplus (e.g. > 1,200 W) or cheap night spot prices are available.
State 4 — Forced Max Thermal Storage 1 : 1 Commands immediate maximum thermal boost during high solar surplus (> 2,500 W) or negative electricity prices.

16. Frequently Asked Questions & Quick Settings Cheat Sheet

Goal / Scenario Where to Go in GEMS Exact Setting to Adjust
Charge my EV using 100% free sunshine only Dashboard → Click EV Charger Circle Select PV Only mode (or Eco with Cheapest Spot Price Hours = 0). Also enable Automatic 1P/3P Phase Switching on your Raedian charger!
Keep my Flanders monthly peak at the 2.50 kW minimum Settings → Strategy Set Optimization Mode = Flanders (Peak Shaving), Capacity Peak Limit = 2.50 kW, and Peak Shaving Buffer = 150 W.
Stop paying penalties when afternoon solar spot prices go negative Settings → Strategy & Devices Check Allow Curtailment on your Solis inverter in Devices, and enable Active Inverter Curtailment in Strategy with Base Export Price Threshold = 0.00 €/kWh.
Protect my Flemish Groenestroomcertificaten (GSC) during negative prices Settings → Strategy Select your GSC Preset (e.g. €250 or €350/MWh). GEMS will only curtail solar if the negative spot price exceeds your subsidy value!
Pre-charge my home battery overnight before a cloudy winter day Settings → Strategy Under Battery Grid Charging, select Dynamic + Solar Forecast and set your Grid Charge Ceiling (€/kWh) (e.g. 0.10 €/kWh) or add a Custom Charging Window.
Heat my water boiler or SG-Ready heat pump with excess solar Settings → Relays & Switches Add a rule with Condition = solar_export_exceeds (e.g. 1500 W) and set Min Run Time = 900 sec.
