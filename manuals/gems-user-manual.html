1. Welcome to GEMS: What It Does & Why It's in Your Home

Welcome to GEMS (Grid Energy Management System)! Installed directly in your electrical panel on a dedicated compact computer (Raspberry Pi 5 with an ultra-reliable solid-state NVMe drive), GEMS is the intelligent central brain of your home energy setup.

In a modern home, you have several powerful electrical systems: solar panels on the roof, an electric vehicle in the driveway, a home battery storage unit, a heat pump or boiler, and everyday kitchen and laundry appliances. Without an energy manager, these systems work completely blindly:

  • Your car might start charging at full speed from the expensive utility grid just as your oven turns on, causing an expensive capacity peak.
  • Your solar panels might inject cheap or even negatively priced electricity into the grid while your home battery sits half empty.
  • Your heat pump might run at night when electricity is pricey instead of pre-heating your water with free solar energy at noon.

GEMS solves all of this automatically. It measures every watt generated and consumed in your home every single second. It directs free solar power straight to where it saves you the most money, caps expensive grid import peaks, and schedules your EV charging during the cheapest hours—all 100% automatically.

🔒 100% Local & Private: Zero Subscriptions, Zero Cloud Dependency

Unlike commercial smart home products that require expensive monthly subscriptions and send your private data to remote servers, GEMS runs entirely inside your own house. If your internet connection goes down, GEMS continues balancing your solar, battery, and car without skipping a beat!

Understanding the PowerFlow Circles (Nodes)

Circle Node Color Badge What It Tells You in Plain Language
Solar ● Gold / Amber The total instantaneous power generated by your roof solar panels (e.g. 4.8 kW). At night, this naturally displays 0 W.
Battery ● Emerald Green Shows whether your home battery is charging (green arrow pointing down into the battery) or discharging (arrow pointing up to supply the home). Also displays the live battery percentage (State of Charge, e.g. 85%).
Grid ● Cobalt Blue Shows your interaction with the utility company. When importing electricity, arrows point inward toward your home. When exporting surplus solar, arrows point outward toward the street.
Home (Load) ● Teal Green The total electricity being used by your household right now (kitchen appliances, lights, TV, computer, ventilation).
EV Charger ● Purple Shows the power flowing into your electric vehicle (e.g. 7.4 kW) and the current charging mode (e.g. ECO or FAST). Only visible when an EV charger is connected.
Relay / Heating ● Orange Shows whether your smart water heater or heat pump is switched ON or OFF. Only visible when smart relays are configured.

What the Animated Flowing Bubbles Mean

The moving colored dots between the circles show the path electricity is taking:

  • Dots moving from Solar → Home: Your house is running on 100% free sunshine!
  • Dots moving from Solar → Battery: Extra solar power is being stored for tonight.
  • Dots moving from Solar → EV Charger: Your electric car is charging directly from your roof for free.
  • Dots moving from Battery → Home: In the evening, your battery is powering your appliances without taking costly power from the grid.
  • Dots moving from Grid → Home: Solar and battery are currently not enough to cover your appliances; the difference is pulled from the utility grid.

5. Managing EV Charging Modes: When to Use Each

GEMS talks directly to your EV charger (using high-speed Modbus TCP or native OCPP). You can change your charging mode at any time with a single tap on the dashboard:

🚗 Which Charging Mode Should You Choose?
1. ECO / Solar Only Mode
When to choose: Sunny weekends, working from home, or whenever the car is parked for several hours.
What GEMS does: Charges strictly using excess solar production. If clouds roll in, charging throttles or pauses automatically. You get 100% free solar kilometers without pulling costly electricity from the grid!
2. FAST / Max Power Mode
When to choose: You arrive home with an empty battery and need to leave again in 1 hour.
What GEMS does: Immediately supplies the maximum rated current your car and fuse box allow (e.g. 11 kW or 22 kW), drawing simultaneously from solar, battery, and grid.
3. SMART Dynamic Mode
When to choose: Cloudy winter days if you have a dynamic electricity contract.
What GEMS does: Automatically schedules charging during the 1 to 8 cheapest spot hours of the night (typically between 01:00 and 05:00) when electricity costs only a few cents.
4. OFF / Paused Mode
When to choose: The car is plugged in, but you do not want it to charge right now (e.g. reserving battery power for household dinner cooking).
💡 Why EV Charging Requires at Least 6 Amps

Under international automotive standards (IEC 61851), electric vehicles cannot charge at less than 6 Amps per phase (approximately 1.4 kW on single-phase, or 4.1 kW on three-phase). In Solar-Only mode, GEMS waits until you have at least 1.4 kW of steady solar surplus before starting charging. If solar drops below this threshold for more than a few minutes, GEMS pauses charging cleanly to prevent drawing from the grid.

💳 Company Cars & Employer Split-Billing Compatibility

Do you drive a company car with an employer charge card or external billing subscription? GEMS connects to your charger via Modbus TCP on your home network. This means GEMS can optimize your charging speed and solar self-consumption without disconnecting your employer's billing service! Both run happily side-by-side.

6. Home Battery Storage & Peak Shaving

Your home battery (such as Huawei LUNA2000, BYD, or Victron) is your personal energy buffer. GEMS manages it automatically 24 hours a day based on three golden rules:

1. Day-to-Night Solar Buffering

During the day, your roof panels often generate more electricity than your refrigerator and lights need. Instead of dumping this power into the street for a tiny feed-in fee, GEMS pours every surplus watt into your home battery. In the evening, when the sun sets and your family turns on the oven, television, and lights, GEMS automatically discharges the battery to keep your grid meter at zero.

2. Peak Shaving: Protecting You from the Flanders Capacity Tariff

In Flanders, your grid network fees are determined by the highest 15-minute average power you draw from the grid each month (the Capaciteitstarief, with a minimum baseline of 2.50 kW). If you turn on the washing machine, tumble dryer, and kettle at the same time, your power demand might spike to 4.5 kW.

How GEMS Protects You: The millisecond your home demand rises toward your 2.50 kW ceiling, GEMS commands your home battery to discharge the difference (2.0 kW). The utility grid meter never sees the spike, keeping your monthly electricity bill at the absolute minimum baseline!

3. Smart Adaptive Ceiling: No Panic If a Peak Occurs

What happens if your battery happens to be empty on a dark winter day and an unavoidable spike reaches 4.2 kW?

In simple systems, the controller would keep strictly limiting you. But in GEMS, the Smart Adaptive Ceiling takes over:

  • GEMS recognizes that because a 4.2 kW peak was already registered in the meter, the network operator will bill you for 4.2 kW for this calendar month anyway.
  • GEMS temporarily elevates its working ceiling to 4.2 kW for the rest of that month!
  • This allows you to charge your car faster and enjoy greater convenience without increasing your bill by a single euro!
  • On midnight on the 1st of the next month, the ceiling automatically drops back down to 2.50 kW.

7. Dynamic Tariffs & Negative Electricity Prices

If you have an hourly dynamic electricity contract (such as TotalEnergies Pixel Dynamic, Mega, Bolt, or Engie Dynamic), your price per kilowatt-hour changes every single hour according to the European EPEX Spot auction.

Figure 7.1: 24-Hour effective retail tariff breakdown curve showing hourly spot prices, network fees, and injection returns.

What are Negative Electricity Prices?

On sunny, windy weekend afternoons, Europe's wind turbines and solar roofs often produce far more electricity than factories and homes are consuming. When this happens, wholesale electricity prices drop below zero: the grid literally pays you to consume electricity!

Conversely, if you export solar power to the grid during negative price hours, you could be charged an injection penalty (you pay the utility company to take your solar power!).

The 4-Step GEMS Protection Strategy During Negative Hours

Whenever electricity prices drop below zero, GEMS automatically steps through a 4-tier financial defense:

  1. Tier 1 — Fill Your Home Battery: GEMS directs all solar power to charge your home battery to 100%. If prices are deeply negative, GEMS even charges your battery from the grid, earning you money while charging!
  2. Tier 2 — Boost Your EV: If your car is plugged in, GEMS switches to maximum charging speed to absorb the cheap or negative energy into your vehicle's large battery.
  3. Tier 3 — Heat Your Hot Water: GEMS activates smart relays connected to your domestic water heater or heat pump buffer tank, storing the cheap energy as hot water for your evening shower.
  4. Tier 4 — Active Inverter Curtailment: If your battery, car, and water tanks are all completely full and exporting would cause a financial loss, GEMS throttles your solar inverter down to match your exact home load, preventing a single cent of export penalties!

8. Flemish Groenestroomcertificaten (GSC) Subsidy Protection

Homeowners in Flanders with solar installations built before 2021 often receive guaranteed Groenestroomcertificaten (GSC) (green certificates) worth €90, €250, €350, or €450 for every 1,000 kWh of solar energy produced.

💶 How GSC Value Protects You From Losing Money

If your solar system receives €250/certificaat (€0.250/kWh) from Fluvius, you earn €0.250 for every kWh your panels generate, regardless of what happens on the grid.

If the wholesale market price drops to -€0.10/kWh, an unsmart system would panic and shut down your solar panels. But GEMS knows your GSC value! It calculates:

Net Return = GSC Subsidy (+€0.250) + Market Price (-€0.100) = +€0.150/kWh Net Profit!

Because you are still making a net profit of 15 cents per kWh, GEMS keeps your solar panels producing at 100%. Curtailment will only activate if the negative price drops below -€0.250/kWh (-250 €/MWh), guaranteeing you never miss a single euro of your hard-earned green subsidies!

9. Belgian Elia Green Grid Sync Boost

GEMS connects directly to the Belgian high-voltage transmission grid operator Elia via live Open Data feeds:

  • GEMS tracks the real-time share of wind and solar electricity powering the Belgian national grid.
  • When the national green share exceeds your configured threshold (e.g. 65% Renewable), GEMS triggers the Green Grid Sync Boost.
  • Your EV charger and battery will prioritize charging during these high-renewable windows, helping stabilize the national power grid while charging your car with genuinely green electricity!

13. Homeowner Frequently Asked Questions & Everyday Tips

Q: What happens if our home internet goes down?

Nothing stops working! Because GEMS runs 100% locally on your Raspberry Pi microcomputer inside your electrical cabinet, solar self-consumption, battery peak shaving, and EV charging controls continue running normally. When internet is restored, GEMS simply fetches the newest Day-Ahead price forecast.

Q: When does my Flanders capacity peak reset?

In Flanders, the Capaciteitstarief billing cycle is measured per calendar month. At midnight on the 1st of every month (00:00), GEMS automatically resets its peak counter and begins tracking the new month's baseline.

Q: How can I access GEMS when I am away from home?

For safety and privacy, GEMS does not expose open ports to the public internet. You can access GEMS securely from outside your home using Raspberry Pi Connect (which requires zero router configuration) or via a private home VPN (such as WireGuard or Tailscale).

Q: Why did my EV stop charging when the sun was still shining?

If your charger is set to ECO / Solar Only mode, your car requires at least 1.4 kW (single-phase) or 4.1 kW (three-phase) of continuous surplus. If heavy domestic appliances (such as an oven or washing machine) turned on, or if heavy clouds rolled in, GEMS temporarily paused your EV to prevent drawing costly power from the utility grid. Charging resumes as soon as solar surplus stabilizes!

Q: Can I adjust settings myself without calling the installer?

Yes! Everyday settings—such as changing your EV charging mode (Eco vs Fast), reviewing energy graphs, downloading PDF reports, or toggling smart relays—can be performed directly from the main dashboard at any time.
