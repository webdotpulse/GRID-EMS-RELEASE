Section 1: Enerlution Inverter & Battery Ecosystem (LV vs. HV)

Unlike single-architecture brands, Enerlution manufactures two distinct voltage classes: 51.2 V Low-Voltage (LV) single-phase storage systems and 150–600 V High-Voltage (HV) three-phase stackable towers. Before installing or wiring any cables, confirm that your battery model matches the exact voltage class of your hybrid inverter.

🛑 CRITICAL VOLTAGE SEPARATION RULE: Never Cross-Wire LV (51.2V) & HV (150–600V)!
  • Architecture A — 51.2 V Low-Voltage (LFPWall-5000, LFPWall-10K-V2, Roller-16K): Wired strictly in 51.2 V parallel (P+ to P+, P- to P-). Connect only to 48V/51.2V Low-Voltage hybrid inverters (Enerlution HY / L3–L6 LV or compatible 48V LV inverters such as Solis -L). Never wire LV wall/roller packs in series!
  • Architecture B — High-Voltage Stackable (GroundHV-2500, GH02-5324 + PDU/BCU): Individual blocks stack in series underneath a dedicated High-Voltage PDU / BCU Control Box (~153 V – 500+ Vdc). Connect only to High-Voltage 3-phase hybrid inverters (Enerlution HYTH-5K..12K / L5–L12 HV). Keep the HV Manual Service Disconnect (MSD) / PDU breaker OFF and wear HV-insulated gloves during assembly!

Section 2: Step-by-Step Battery & Smart Meter Wiring Manual

De-energize the AC distribution breaker, PV DC isolator, external DC breaker / HV PDU switch, and all battery module power buttons before connecting any power or communication leads.

2.1 Both Wiring Topologies: LV 51.2V Parallel vs. HV Stackable Tower

Left: Architecture A — 51.2V LV Parallel (LFPWall / Roller)  |  Right: Architecture B — HV Stackable (GroundHV / GH02 + PDU)

Architecture A — Wiring 51.2V LV Batteries (LFPWall-5000, LFPWall-10K-V2, Roller-16K)

  1. External 2-Pole Non-Polarized DC MCCB: Install a non-polarized 2-pole DC breaker or gBat DC fuse disconnector (≥ 80 Vdc; 100–125 A for a single LFPWall-5000, 160–200 A for LFPWall-10K-V2 or Roller-16K) between the battery bank and the inverter BAT+ / BAT- lugs.
  2. Parallel Balancing Rule: For 2–3 parallel packs, connect equal-length bridge cables (P+ to P+, P- to P-) and take BAT+ from Pack 1 (Master) P+ and BAT- diagonally from the final pack's P-. For 4+ parallel packs, run identical-length 25–35 mm² fused branch leads from each battery to rated 250–400 A tin-plated copper DC busbars.
  3. Protective Earth (⏚ PE): Bond each wall/roller chassis ⏚ M6 ground terminal directly to the inverter PE bar with ≥ 6 mm² green/yellow copper conductor.

Architecture B — Wiring High-Voltage Towers (GroundHV-2500 / GH02-5324 + PDU/BCU)

  1. Mechanical Stacking & Wall Anchoring: Level the steel base plinth against a load-bearing wall. Stack the required number of HV battery bricks vertically (minimum 3–4 × GroundHV-2500 bricks = ~153–204 Vdc to exceed HYTH start voltage), fasten the anti-tip side locking brackets between every tier, and seat the PDU / BCU High-Voltage Control Head on top.
  2. HV DC Plugs & Safety Interlock: Verify the PDU HV circuit breaker is OFF and any orange Manual Service Disconnect (MSD) safety plug is removed. Route double-insulated 6–10 mm² (1000 Vdc) HV storage cables from the PDU HV+ / HV- touch-safe connectors into the HYTH inverter's BAT+ / BAT- ports until the locking collars click, and bond the base + PDU ⏚ PE studs to the inverter earth bar.

2.2 BMS CAN Cable Pinout & Multi-Pack DIP Switch Addressing

Enerlution batteries establish closed-loop BMS communication (SOC %, SOH %, pack voltage, cell temperature derating, and charge/discharge current envelopes) over CAN bus via an RJ45 cable linking the Master Battery / PDU CAN port to the Enerlution inverter BMS / CAN port:

RJ45 Pin T568B Wire Color Enerlution Battery / PDU Port Inverter BMS / CAN Port Wiring Notes
Pin 1 & 2 W-Orange / Orange RS485-B / RS485-A (Optional) RS485-B / RS485-A Unused in CAN mode
Pin 3 & 6 W-Green / Green Signal GND Signal GND Reference Ground
Pin 4 Solid Blue CAN-H (CAN High) CAN-H (CAN High) Mandatory CAN-H
Pin 5 White / Blue CAN-L (CAN Low) CAN-L (CAN Low) Mandatory CAN-L
Pin 7 & 8 W-Brown / Brown Reserved / Shield GND Reserved / Shield GND Check factory lead label
  • Parallel Link Cables & DIP Switches (Set Strictly While Powered OFF):
    • Single Battery Pack: Keep the ADD DIP switches in factory standalone position (0000 all OFF, or Address 1 per enclosed V1/V2 BMS sheet).
    • Multiple Parallel LV Packs (LFPWall / Roller): Daisy-chain CAT5e/CAT6 link cords from Master Pack 1 Link Out / RS485-B → Slave Pack 2 Link In / RS485-A → Slave Pack 3 Link In. Assign sequential binary addresses while powered down: Master (Pack 1) = Address 1 (ON OFF OFF OFF), Slave 1 (Pack 2) = Address 2 (OFF ON OFF OFF), Slave 2 (Pack 3) = Address 3 (ON ON OFF OFF), Slave 3 (Pack 4) = Address 4 (OFF OFF ON OFF). If your DIP block includes a dedicated termination bit on the last pole, set termination ON on the physically farthest pack.
    • Pairing 51.2V Enerlution Packs with Third-Party LV Inverters (e.g. Solis): Ensure the Enerlution BMS protocol matches the target inverter (Pylon / Solis CAN over Pins 4 & 5; switchable via DIP or Enerlution's USB-RS485 BMS Monitor utility if factory-shipped on a different OEM profile).

2.4 Safe Soft-Start Power-Up & Shutdown Sequence

⚡ Mandatory Pre-Charge Startup Order
  1. Verify Polarity Cold: With all breakers OFF, verify BAT+ / BAT- polarity with a DC voltmeter.
  2. Close DC Isolator First: Switch the LV 2-pole DC MCCB ON (or insert the HV MSD safety plug and switch the PDU HV breaker ON) while the BMS is still asleep.
  3. Wake Master / PDU BMS: Press and hold the Master battery or HV PDU POWER / SW button (2–3 seconds) so the internal pre-charge circuit softly energizes the inverter capacitors. Confirm green RUN LED is solid/blinking normally and red ALM LED is OFF.
  4. Energize PV & Grid: Switch the inverter PV DC isolator ON, then turn ON the AC Grid breaker.
Shutdown (Reverse): AC Breaker OFF → PV DC Switch OFF → Hold Master/PDU POWER button 3s to sleep BMS → DC MCCB / PDU Breaker OFF (wait 5 minutes before touching HV terminals!).

3.1 Connecting Locally via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Access Point

  1. Plug the Enerlution Wi-Fi / LAN communication module firmly into the bottom COM / Wi-Fi port of the inverter.
  2. Enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your smartphone (temporarily turn Mobile Data OFF if joining the module's local Wi-Fi AP SSID matching the logger serial number so your phone does not drop offline traffic).
  3. Open the Enerlution App, select Local Commissioning / Device Debugging, log in with your Installer credentials, and bind the communication stick to your local 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or wired Ethernet LAN (reserve a static DHCP IPv4 address on your router!).

5.1 Regional Optimization Recipes (Settings → Strategy)

🇧🇪 Recipe A: Flanders (Peak Shaving)

  • Mode: Flanders Mode (Peak Shaving)
  • Capacity Peak Limit: 2.5 kW (standard) or 3.0–3.5 kW (heat pump + EV).
  • Peak Shaving Buffer: 200 W
  • Active Curtailment: Checked (✓), Base 0.00 €/kWh
  • GSC Subsidy Shield: Enter €0 (modern PV) or exact certificate value (€250–€450/MWh). GEMS automatically keeps Enerlution solar exporting down to -GSC/1000 €/kWh!
  • How It Works: Enerlution's Self-Consumption loop discharges the battery to cover cooking/heat-pump spikes while GEMS throttles EV charging before any 15-min billing peak is breached.

🇳🇱 Recipe B: Netherlands (Saldering)

  • Mode: Netherlands Mode (Smart Saldering)
  • Active Curtailment: Checked (✓)
  • Base Export Price Threshold: 0.00 €/kWh (or your supplier's feed-in levy threshold).
  • How It Works: Daytime PV surplus fills your Enerlution LFPWall / GroundHV stack first, then ramps Raedian EV charging (6A–32A) and SG-Ready hot water. Once storage is 100% full during negative-return hours, GEMS regulates Enerlution output in 1-Watt steps so grid export drops cleanly to 0 W!

🌿 Recipe C: Fixed / Dual-Tariff Eco

  • Mode: Eco (Max Self-Consumption)
  • Active Curtailment: Unchecked (☐) (unless on a contract with negative export penalties).
  • Grid Fuse Protection: Enter exact main breaker amps (25A / 32A / 40A) and Meter Offline Fallback = 0 A.
  • How It Works: Pure autonomous solar self-consumption: Enerlution prioritizes battery charging, GEMS steers surplus solar into EVs and thermal relays, and main fuses are protected 24/7.

5.2 Coordinating Enerlution Storage with EV Chargers (Raedian Wallboxes)

  • Daytime Solar Surplus Priority: Set your Raedian NEO / NEX / Gemini wallbox to PV Only (Solar) or Eco on the GEMS Dashboard. Because Enerlution's behind-the-meter loop absorbs initial PV surplus into the battery first, GEMS automatically waits until excess solar exceeds your configured EV threshold (e.g. 1.4 kW 1-phase) before ramping EV charging—preventing the EV charger from stealing solar away from an empty home battery or seesawing against it!
  • Smart Cheapest-Hours EV Charging: Enable Smart EV Cheapest Hours (Settings → Strategy, e.g. 3–4 cheapest hours) alongside your Energy Contract formula (Settings → Energy Contract). If you schedule high-power overnight EV charging and want to preserve your Enerlution battery for the morning household peak, either schedule EV charging during windows when the Enerlution App's optional Discharge Window is paused or cap EV charging current below your Flanders capacity ceiling.

Section 6: Verification Checklist & Troubleshooting Matrix

6.1 6-Point Post-Commissioning Handover Checklist

✅ Mandatory Field Verification Before Leaving Site
  1. BMS & SOH Verification: Confirm zero red ALM LEDs on all LFPWall, Roller, or GroundHV modules and verify live SOC % and SOH % appear in the Enerlution App (Battery State = Charging / Discharging / Standby).
  2. GEMS Online & Debug Modal: In Settings → Connected Devices, confirm Enerlution Hybrid Inverter displays ● Online. Click Debug and verify ~230 V grid voltage, plausible PV watts, and live Battery SOC %.
  3. 1-Watt Curtailment Release Check: Verify in the Logger tab that during normal positive-price operation GEMS logs Released active power limit (40400=0) with zero write errors.
  4. Kettle Load Polarity Check: Boil a ~2 kW kettle and confirm Home Consumption increases by ~2 kW on the GEMS Dashboard while the Enerlution battery smoothly ramps discharge to cover the load.
  5. Export Configuration Backup: In Settings → System Info, click Export Configuration Backup (.json).
  6. Lock Certified Installer Mode: Click the padlock icon 🔒 in the Settings header to lock critical device and breaker settings.
