1. Executive Overview & Unified Hardware Abstraction Layer

GEMS (Grid Energy Management System) is an edge Energy Management System engineered specifically to execute on local Raspberry Pi hardware without reliance on external cloud servers. The entire system is local-first: all automated power balancing, solar surplus tracking, Flanders peak shaving, and main fuse protection algorithms run directly inside the local Go binary (gems daemon), querying connected hardware and recalculating setpoints every single second.

Unified Hardware Interfaces (Go Backend)

Rather than hardcoding ad-hoc device logic, every hardware driver in backend/internal/templates/ implements one or more universal interfaces defined in backend/internal/models/models.go:

DevicePoller (Universal Base)

Implemented by all 140 templates. Standardizes Connect(), Poll(), Status(), and Close(). Standardizes real-time power (W), battery power (W), grid flow (W), total energy (kWh), and battery SOC (%).

ChargeController (EV Chargers)

Provides SetChargeCurrent(amps float64) (dynamic throttling from 6A to 32A in 1A steps) and SetPhaseMode(phases int) (automated switching between 1-phase and 3-phase charging modes).

BatteryController (Inverters & BESS)

Provides ChargeBattery(powerW float64) and DischargeBattery(powerW float64) for automated Day-Ahead EPEX spot price arbitrage and Flemish capacity tariff peak shaving.

InverterController (Solar Generation)

Provides SetActivePowerLimit(powerW float64) for automated solar curtailment during negative electricity spot market hours and Dutch zero-export regulatory compliance.

RelayController (Smart Switches)

Provides SetState(on bool) for automated potential-free contactor actuation, powering domestic hot water (DHW) immersion heaters and triggering SG-Ready heat pump boost states.

PhasePoller & PhaseVoltagePoller

Returns sub-second per-phase currents (IL1, IL2, IL3) and voltages (VL1, VL2, VL3) for dynamic phase balancing and main grid breaker protection.

MeterPeakPoller (Belgian Capacity Tariff)

Implemented by P1 smart meters. Reads the official 15-minute rolling average peak demand register (W) directly from Fluvius DSMR meters to enforce capacity tariff ceilings.

MultiConnectorPoller (Dual-Socket EVSE)

Implemented by dual-socket chargers (e.g. RAEDIAN Gemini). Manages independent telemetry, sessions, and charging currents across multiple physical charging ports simultaneously.

2. What GEMS Actually Does With Devices (Strategy Engine)

GEMS integrates disparate hardware from dozens of manufacturers into a cohesive, intelligent energy ecosystem. Below are the 9 automated closed-loop strategies executed by the GEMS control loop (backend/strategy.go):

1. 🌞 Eco Mode (100% Solar Self-Consumption)

Continuously computes instantaneous solar surplus (Psurplus = Psolar − Phouse). Surpluses are routed sequentially: first charging the home battery, then modulating EV charge currents (6A–32A), and finally triggering hot water immersion heaters or heat pump SG-Ready boost states.

2. 🇧🇪 Flanders Capacity Tariff & Dynamic Ceiling

Monitors household demand every second and computes projected 15-minute rolling peak imports. When demand approaches the monthly ceiling, GEMS throttles EV charging to 6A or 0A, disengages non-critical relays, and commands battery discharge. Features an Adaptive Ceiling: if an unavoidable peak occurs (e.g. 4.8 kW from cooking), GEMS raises its operational ceiling to that level for the remainder of the month so the car charges faster at zero extra tariff cost.

3. 🇳🇱 Netherlands Zero-Export & Curtailment

Protects Dutch installations against grid export penalties and salderingsregeling phase-outs. GEMS commands solar inverters to reduce generation (SetActivePowerLimit) whenever export limits are exceeded and storage batteries are saturated.

4. 💶 Negative Spot Protection & Flemish GSC Shield

Protects homeowners with dynamic EPEX Day-Ahead energy tariffs from paying export penalties during negative electricity pricing. In Flanders, GEMS applies an automated GSC Subsidy Shield (e.g. €250/MWh): solar export remains active down to −€0.250/kWh, curtailing only if the market penalty strictly exceeds the value of the green certificate.

5. 🔋 EPEX Day-Ahead Dynamic Battery Arbitrage

Integrates 24-hour EPEX Spot Day-Ahead prices (updated daily at 14:00 CET) with Open-Meteo solar forecasts. Automatically commands the home battery to force-charge during negative/cheapest off-peak hours and discharge during evening peak price windows.

6. 🚗 Smart EV Charging (Cheapest Hours Boost)

Scans the 24-hour EPEX tariff curve to identify the N cheapest hours of the day. Automatically ramps EV charging to maximum power (16A/32A) during those hours, throttling down to 0A during expensive price peaks.

7. ⚡ Dynamic Phase Load Balancing & Fuse Protection

Continuously inspects individual phase currents (IL1, IL2, IL3) against the main grid connection breaker limit (e.g. 25A, 32A, 40A, 63A). If any individual phase exceeds 90% of the breaker rating, GEMS instantly throttles the EV charger within milliseconds to prevent tripping the main fuse.

8. 🔌 Dynamic Phase Switching (1-Phase / 3-Phase)

EV charging requires a minimum current of 6A (1.4 kW on 1-phase; 4.1 kW on 3-phase). For supported chargers (e.g. RAEDIAN, go-e, Alfen, Mennekes, Peblar, Huawei), GEMS commands 1-phase mode during modest solar surplus (1.4 kW–4.1 kW) and automatically switches to 3-phase mode when surplus exceeds 4.2 kW.

9. 🚰 Thermal Storage & SG-Ready Heat Pump Boost

Actuates contactors and smart plugs for domestic hot water (DHW) boilers or triggers SG-Ready Heat Pump Operating State 3 (Boost Mode) during solar excess or negative price windows. Enforces strict minimum run-time and off-time hysteresis to safeguard heat pump compressors against short-cycling.

🔒 Local Fail-Safe Watchdogs

All writable setpoints (EV current, inverter power limits, battery discharge) feature built-in hardware watchdog timeouts. If communication is lost, devices automatically revert to safe autonomous default modes.

5. Communication Protocols & Field Wiring Reference

Connecting physical energy devices to the GEMS Raspberry Pi requires standard industrial interfaces. Below is the reference wiring and protocol architecture:

1. Modbus TCP & Modbus RTU (RS485)

Modbus TCP (Ethernet / Wi-Fi)

Standard industrial port 502 (or 5020/8899). Connects directly via local LAN. Supports simultaneous pollers and sub-second update latency. Zero wiring required when devices reside on the same local subnet.

Modbus RTU RS485 (Serial USB Dongle)

Requires shielded twisted-pair cable (A+, B−, GND). Connect in a strict daisy-chain topology. Terminate both ends of the bus with a 120Ω resistor. Set unique Slave IDs for each device on the same bus.

2. Local OCPP 1.6-J / 2.0.1 Server (EVSE)

GEMS operates a high-performance, embedded local OCPP Central System on port 8887. No external cloud subscription or third-party backend is needed for residential installations:

Local OCPP Server Connection Endpoint

ws://<GEMS-IP>:8887/<ChargePointID>   (or wss:// for TLS connections)

When configured, the EV charger sends real-time MeterValues (W, Wh, Amps, Volts) and receives dynamic SetChargingProfile setpoints calculated every second by the GEMS control loop.

3. P1 Port DSMR Smart Meters (Fluvius, Dutch Smart Meter)

Direct connection from the digital meter's RJ12 P1 port into the GEMS Raspberry Pi via USB-to-RJ12 cable:

Pin 2 Data Request Active-High Request Line Tied to +5V (pull-up) to continuously enable telemetry output
