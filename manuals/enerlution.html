Enerlution Hybrid Inverters & LiFePO4 Battery Storage Guide

Complete reference manual for wiring Enerlution 51.2V Low-Voltage (LFPWall-5000, LFPWall-10K-V2, Roller-16K) and High-Voltage Stackable (GroundHV-2500, GH02-5324 + PDU/BCU) batteries to Enerlution HY & HYTH Hybrid Inverters, commissioning the Enerlution App, and optimizing GRID-EMS (GEMS) for 1-Watt curtailment, Flanders peak shaving, and EV charging.

1. Enerlution LV vs. HV Ecosystem & Compatibility Matrix

Enerlution produces both 51.2 V Low-Voltage (LV) parallel battery packs for single-phase installations and 150–600 V High-Voltage (HV) stackable battery towers for three-phase systems. Always verify that your battery voltage class matches your inverter model before starting installation.

CRITICAL VOLTAGE SEPARATION GUARDRAIL — Never Cross-Wire LV (51.2V) & HV (150–600V):
  • Architecture A — 51.2 V Low-Voltage (LFPWall-5000, LFPWall-10K-V2, Roller-16K): Wire multi-pack banks strictly in 51.2 V parallel (P+ to P+, P- to P-). Connect only to 48V/51.2V Low-Voltage hybrid inverters (Enerlution HY / L3–L6 LV or compatible 48V LV inverters such as Solis -L). Never wire LV wall/roller packs in series!
  • Architecture B — High-Voltage Stackable (GroundHV-2500, GH02-5324 + PDU/BCU): Bricks stack in series under a dedicated High-Voltage PDU / BCU Control Head (~153 V – 500+ Vdc). Connect only to 3-phase High-Voltage hybrid inverters (Enerlution HYTH-5K..12K / L5–L12 HV). Keep the HV Manual Service Disconnect (MSD) and PDU breaker OFF during assembly!

2. Step-by-Step Battery & Smart Meter Wiring Manual

Complete all DC power, protective earth, DIP-switch, CAN, and smart meter wiring with the AC Grid breaker, PV DC isolator, external DC MCCB / HV PDU breaker, and all battery power switches turned strictly OFF.

2.1 LV 51.2V Parallel Wiring vs. HV Stackable Tower Assembly

  • Architecture A — 51.2V Low-Voltage Parallel (LFPWall-5000, LFPWall-10K-V2, Roller-16K):
    1. Install a dedicated non-polarized 2-pole DC MCCB or gBat DC fuse isolator (≥ 80 Vdc, 125–200 A) between the battery bank and the inverter BAT+ / BAT- lugs. Never use polarized AC breakers on bidirectional battery circuits.
    2. 2 to 3 Parallel Packs (Diagonal Takeoff): Connect equal-length jumper cables (P+ to P+, P- to P-). Connect the main inverter BAT+ lead to Pack 1 (Master) P+, and connect the main inverter BAT- lead diagonally to the LAST Pack's P- so loop impedance is identical across all packs.
    3. 4+ Parallel Packs (Copper DC Busbar): Run identical-length 25–35 mm² fused branch cables from each battery to rated 250–400 A tin-plated copper DC busbars, then feed the inverter from the busbars.
    4. Bond every battery chassis ground stud (⏚ PE) to the inverter PE bar using ≥ 6 mm² green/yellow copper conductor.
  • Architecture B — High-Voltage Series Towers (GroundHV-2500 / GH02-5324 + PDU/BCU):
    1. Anchor the level base plinth against a load-bearing wall. Stack the HV battery modules vertically (minimum 3–4 × GroundHV-2500 modules = ~153–204 Vdc to exceed HYTH startup voltage), lock anti-tip side brackets between tiers, and seat the PDU / BCU Control Head on top.
    2. With the PDU HV breaker OFF and orange Manual Service Disconnect (MSD) plug removed, connect double-insulated 6–10 mm² (1000 Vdc) HV leads from PDU HV+ / HV- into the HYTH inverter BAT+ / BAT- terminals until the locking collars click, and bond chassis ⏚ PE to the inverter earth bar.

  • Parallel LV Link Cables & Cold DIP Switch Addressing: Daisy-chain CAT5e/CAT6 cords from Master Pack 1 Link Out / RS485-B → Slave Pack 2 Link In / RS485-A → Slave Pack 3 Link In. Set binary ADD DIP switches strictly while powered OFF: standalone pack = 0000 (or Address 1); multi-pack array = Master Pack 1: ON OFF OFF OFF (Addr 1), Slave Pack 2: OFF ON OFF OFF (Addr 2), Slave Pack 3: ON ON OFF OFF (Addr 3), Slave Pack 4: OFF OFF ON OFF (Addr 4).
  • External RS-485 Smart Meter Terminals (9600 bps, 8N1, Address 1):
    • CHINT DTSU666 (3-Phase): Terminal 24 = RS485-A (+), Terminal 25 = RS485-B (-).
    • CHINT DDSU666 (1-Phase): Terminal 5 = RS485-A (+), Terminal 6 = RS485-B (-).
    • Eastron SDM630-Modbus V2: Terminal A / 485A = RS485-A (+), Terminal B / 485B = RS485-B (-).
    • Clamp CTs immediately after the utility meter with arrows pointing Grid → Home/Load.
  • Mandatory Soft-Start Pre-Charge Sequence: (1) Verify cold DC polarity with a voltmeter → (2) Close the LV 2-pole DC MCCB (or insert HV MSD plug & close PDU HV breaker) while BMS is asleep → (3) Hold Master/PDU POWER / SW button 2–3s so internal pre-charge resistors energize the inverter DC bus smoothly (RUN green, ALM off) → (4) Turn ON PV DC isolator → (5) Turn ON AC Grid breaker.

4. Onboarding Enerlution in GEMS

Unlock Certified Installer Mode (padlock icon 🔒 in Settings), onboard your utility P1 Meter (p1_serial) first, then click Scanner → Scan Subnet (GEMS auto-detects Enerlution IEEE MAC prefixes 70:B3:D5 and E4:5F:01 plus ASCII model/serial registers) or add manually under Settings → Devices → + Add Device → Inverter / Solar → Enerlution Hybrid Inverter (enerlution_inverter):

  • Endpoint & Bus: Enter reserved static IPv4 on Port 502 (or /dev/serial/by-id/... at 9600 bps, 8N1) with Modbus ID = 1 and Poll Interval = 3s (LAN/RS-485) or 5s (2.4 GHz Wi-Fi).
  • Rated Power & Curtailment: Enter exact inverter AC nameplate kW (e.g. 5.0, 6.0, 8.0, 10.0, 12.0) and check Allow Curtailment so GEMS can regulate solar output in 1-Watt increments (40400 / 40441).
  • Phase, Meter & Battery Flags: Select exact Phase Wiring (L1/L2/L3 on 1P HY or L1-L2-L3 on 3P HYTH), check Grid Meter Connected? if a DTSU666/SDM630 is wired, check Battery Connected?, and enter exact total battery capacity in kWh (5.2, 10.4, 16.1, 10.0, 15.0...). (Note: If an Enerlution 51.2V LV battery is wired to a Solis LV inverter instead, select the Solis template in GEMS).

5. Optimal GEMS Strategy & Raedian EV Coordination

  1. Flanders Mode (Peak Shaving + GSC Protection): Set Capacity Peak Limit = 2.5 kW (or site target) and Buffer = 200 W. Enerlution's internal Self-Consumption loop discharges the battery to cover household spikes while GEMS throttles EV charging before any 15-minute billing peak is breached. Enter your Green Certificate (GSC) value (€/MWh) so GEMS keeps solar exporting down to -GSC/1000 €/kWh.
  2. Netherlands Mode (Smart Saldering & 1-Watt Zero-Export): Enable Active Inverter Curtailment (0.00 €/kWh threshold). Daytime solar fills your Enerlution battery first, then feeds EVs and thermal relays, and once storage reaches 100% during negative spot prices GEMS regulates Enerlution output in stepless 1-Watt steps so grid export drops cleanly to 0 W.
  3. Raedian EV Wallbox Coordination: Set your Raedian NEO / NEX / Gemini charger to PV Only (Solar) or Eco. Because Enerlution absorbs initial solar surplus into the home battery first, GEMS waits until excess export exceeds your EV start threshold before ramping EV current—preventing seesawing between home battery and EV!

6. Quick Troubleshooting Reference

Symptom Probable Cause Resolution
Battery State reads Offline (7) or Fault (6) CAN cable in RS485/Link socket, wrong BMS protocol, or DIP switches changed while ON Power OFF all modules, set cold binary ADD DIP switches, connect RJ45 (Pin 4 CAN-H, Pin 5 CAN-L) from Master/PDU CAN to Inverter BMS, and select Lithium-Ion (CAN) in the Enerlution App.
HV HYTH inverter does not detect GroundHV-2500 Too few series HV modules, MSD safety plug open, or PDU HV breaker OFF Install minimum required HV bricks (≥ 3–4 blocks), lock orange MSD plug, close PDU HV breaker, then hold PDU POWER 2–3s.
PV capped even during positive spot prices Static Zero-Export left enabled in Enerlution App or Rated Power (kW) wrong in GEMS Set Active Power / Export Control = Disabled / Unlimited in the Enerlution App and verify exact AC nameplate kW on the GEMS device card.
Battery charges from AC when house load turns ON DTSU666 / SDM630 CT clamps reversed or phase/RS-485 wires swapped Orient CT arrows strictly Grid → Home/Load and verify DTSU666 Pins 24 (A+) & 25 (B-).
