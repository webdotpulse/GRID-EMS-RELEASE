1. Hardware Architecture & Bill of Materials (BOM)

GEMS is an autonomous, lightweight edge controller engineered in Go (Golang) and Vue 3. It runs entirely on the customer's local network without requiring cloud subscription dependencies. To eliminate the historical failure point of MicroSD card wear in electrical cabinets, GEMS operates exclusively from an M.2 NVMe SSD utilizing SQLite configured in Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) mode.

Reference Hardware Bill of Materials

Component Specification & Part Reference Field Selection Guidance & Trade-offs
Host Single Board Computer Raspberry Pi 5 (4GB or 8GB RAM, Broadcom BCM2712 Quad-core Cortex-A76) Why Pi 5: Features an exposed 16-pin PCIe 2.0 interface. Memory footprint remains < 150MB RAM during 100 concurrent Modbus polls. 4GB is ideal; 8GB provides extra headroom for local telemetry history.
Internal Solid State Drive 128GB to 512GB M.2 2280 or 2230/2242 PCIe NVMe SSD (TLC / 3D NAND) Why NVMe: Delivers 450+ MB/s sequential write speed and 50,000+ IOPS. Eliminates flash wear from high-frequency time-series database logging. Do not use MicroSD cards for production installations.
M.2 PCIe HAT Adapter Official Raspberry Pi M.2 HAT+ or Pimoroni NVMe Base Mounts directly atop the 40-pin GPIO header or beneath the Pi. Ensure the 16-pin FPC ribbon cable is seated with gold pins oriented toward the board contacts.
Power Supply Unit (PSU) Official Raspberry Pi 27W USB-C PD (5.1V / 5.0A Power Delivery) Critical: Standard 15W phone chargers will cause under-voltage brownouts when the NVMe drive draws peak write current.
Enclosure & DIN Mounting Aluminium Armor Enclosure with Active Cooler fan + DIN-Rail adapter clip Sustains core temperature below 48°C inside closed distribution panels. Active Cooler fan speed is managed dynamically via PWM by the Linux kernel.
Serial Fieldbus Adapter FTDI FT232R / FT232RL USB-to-RS485 with 2.5 kV Galvanic Isolation or DIY P1 cable Required for Modbus RTU (Eastron, Carlo Gavazzi) or direct RJ12 P1 port reading. Galvanic isolation prevents ground loops between the inverter and the Pi.

2. NVMe Bootloader Setup & EEPROM Flashing

Factory Raspberry Pi 5 units ship configured to boot solely from MicroSD (boot order 0xf41). When booting directly from an M.2 NVMe SSD without a MicroSD card inserted, the Raspberry Pi EEPROM must be updated.

📋 Decision Guide: How Should You Prepare the Hardware?
Option 1: In the Workshop (Fastest)
Use a USB M.2 NVMe enclosure connected to your laptop. Flash the pre-configured OS image directly to the SSD using BalenaEtcher or Raspberry Pi Imager. Takes 2 minutes.
Option 2: On-Site in the Field
Keep a spare MicroSD card containing the Raspberry Pi EEPROM boot utility in your toolbag. Boot the Pi once with this SD card to permanently enable PCIe NVMe booting.
🌟 3-Minute EEPROM Setup Procedure
  1. Insert any temporary MicroSD card into your laptop.
  2. Open Raspberry Pi Imager → Choose Device: Raspberry Pi 5.
  3. Choose OS: Misc utility images → Bootloader → NVMe/PCIe Boot.
  4. Select the MicroSD card, click Write, and insert into the Pi 5.
  5. Power on with the official 27W power supply. Within 5 seconds, the green ACT LED will flash rapidly and the HDMI screen will turn solid green.
  6. Disconnect power and remove the MicroSD card. The Pi 5 is now permanently set to BOOT_ORDER=0xf461 and will automatically boot from the NVMe SSD!

Enabling PCIe Gen 3.0 Speeds (Optional Workshop Optimization)

By default, the Pi 5 operates PCIe at Gen 2.0 speeds (500 MB/s). To unlock PCIe Gen 3.0 (up to 850 MB/s), edit /boot/firmware/config.txt and add:

# Enable PCIe Gen 3.0 speeds
dtparam=pciex1
dtparam=pciex1_gen=3

5. Subnet Hardware Discovery (The Scanner)

Instead of guessing IP addresses assigned by the customer's router, use the built-in Scanner in the primary navigation sidebar.

Figure 5.1: Subnet Scanner identifying solar inverters, smart meters, and EV chargers on the local /24 subnet.

How the Scanner Operates

When you click "Scan Subnet", GEMS initiates a parallel sweep across the local /24 subnet (254 host addresses):

  1. ARP Resolution: Queries MAC addresses and resolves them against IEEE vendor OUI registries (e.g. Huawei Technologies, SMA Solar Technology, Shelly / Allterco, Alfen).
  2. Port Probing: Tests open TCP ports associated with energy hardware:
    • Port 502 / 1502: Modbus TCP (Inverters, Batteries, EV Chargers, Eastron meters).
    • Port 80 / 8080 / 443: REST APIs (Shelly relays, HomeWizard P1 meters, Fronius Solar API).
    • Port 8887: GEMS embedded OCPP WebSocket server.
  3. One-Click Onboarding: Clicking "Add Device" next to any detected host automatically pre-fills the IP address, Port, and relevant template in the Device Wizard!

⚡ Decision Guide: Modbus TCP vs OCPP for EV Chargers
Modbus TCP (Recommended & Preferred)
Sub-second response: Throttles from 6A to 32A in under 200ms.
Employer billing coexistence: Does NOT interfere with the charger's cloud OCPP connection (external CPO billing services). Both work simultaneously!
Native OCPP 1.6-J / 2.0.1 (Alternative)
Use when the charger does not support Modbus TCP, or when no third-party cloud billing service is needed. The charger points its WebSocket directly to GEMS on port 8887.

9. Optimization Strategy & Flanders Peak Shaving

Navigate to Settings → Strategy to configure the core optimization engine.

Figure 9.1: Optimization Mode, Flanders Capacity Peak Shaving, and Smart Adaptive Ceiling.

The Three Core Strategy Modes

🎯 Choosing the Right Strategy Mode
Mode 1: Eco (Max Self-Consumption)
Best for: Wallonia, Brussels, Germany, or regions without capacity tariffs.
Stores 100% of excess solar yield into the home battery and electric vehicle. Minimizes grid import without enforcing peak kW power ceilings.
Mode 2: Flanders Mode (Peak Shaving)
Best for: Flanders (Belgium).
Enforces the 15-minute rolling average Capaciteitstarief ceiling (default 2.50 kW). Discharges battery and throttles EV charging whenever household appliances breach the target peak.
Mode 3: Netherlands Mode (Smart Saldering)
Best for: The Netherlands.
Actively monitors dynamic feed-in fees (terugleverkosten) and prioritizes local storage over grid injection during low or negative price intervals.

Flanders Capacity Tariff Parameters (Capaciteitstarief)

Parameter Recommended Value Installer Explanation & Operation
capacity_peak_limit_kw 2.50 kW The baseline monthly peak ceiling. In Flanders, Fluvius bills a minimum capacity baseline of 2.50 kW regardless of consumption. Keeping peaks at or below 2.50 kW minimizes the distribution bill.
peak_shaving_buffer_w 200 W Proactive safety margin subtracted from the peak ceiling (e.g. 2.50 kW - 200W = 2.30 kW trigger). Enables the EMS to start throttling EV charging or discharging batteries before the 15-minute meter average is breached.
peak_shaving_rampup_w 150 W Hysteresis deadband power drop required below the ceiling before re-accelerating throttled EV chargers. Prevents rapid on/off cycling of EV charging relays.
Smart Adaptive Ceiling Automatic (Enabled) If an unavoidable heavy household peak occurs (e.g. oven + heat pump simultaneously reaching 4.2 kW for 15 minutes), GEMS dynamically adapts its operational ceiling to 4.2 kW for the rest of that calendar month. Because the customer will already be billed for 4.2 kW by Fluvius, GEMS allows faster EV charging and greater convenience without increasing the electricity bill by a single cent! On the 1st of the next month, the ceiling automatically resets to 2.50 kW.

10. Active Inverter Curtailment & Flemish GSC Protection

Under dynamic contracts, European wholesale power prices occasionally drop below zero during sunny weekend afternoons. Exporting power during negative price hours incurs feed-in penalties. However, legacy Flemish PV systems receive guaranteed Groenestroomcertificaten (GSC) subsidies.

⚠️ Why Blindly Shutting Off Inverters Destroys Customer Earnings

Simple energy managers shut down inverters as soon as the grid feed-in price drops below €0.00/kWh. If a customer receives €250/certificaat (€0.250/kWh) in Flemish green certificates, shutting down the inverter when the price is -€0.05/kWh causes the homeowner to lose +€0.20/kWh in net profit!

The GSC Mathematical Formula

Effective Curtailment Threshold (€/kWh) = Base Threshold - (GSC Value €/MWh / 1000.0)

Example (€250 GSC):
Effective Threshold = €0.00 - (€250.0 / 1000.0) = -0.250 €/kWh (-250.0 €/MWh)
Flemish GSC Preset Subsidy Value per kWh Effective Curtailment Threshold Installer Action
Geen GSC (€0) €0.000 / kWh ≤ 0.0 €/MWh (0.000 €/kWh) Select for systems installed after 2021 without GSC subsidies. Curtailed immediately when price drops below zero.
€90 GSC €0.090 / kWh ≤ -90.0 €/MWh (-0.090 €/kWh) Inverter continues exporting during negative prices down to -€0.090/kWh. Curtailed only below -€0.090/kWh.
€250 GSC €0.250 / kWh ≤ -250.0 €/MWh (-0.250 €/kWh) Protects legacy Flemish PV installations (2009–2012). Full production maintained through almost all negative hours!
€350 GSC €0.350 / kWh ≤ -350.0 €/MWh (-0.350 €/kWh) Protects high-value legacy installations. Production maintained down to -350 €/MWh.
€450 GSC €0.450 / kWh ≤ -450.0 €/MWh (-0.450 €/kWh) Protects early 2006–2008 Flemish PV installations. Maximum subsidy revenue guaranteed.

Installer Telecontrol Compliance Simulation Controls

Installers can verify plant emergency response directly in the GEMS UI without waiting for grid operator test signals:

  • 🚨 Trip Plant (ASDU 45 Disconnect): Simulates an emergency disconnect command. GEMS sends an instantaneous zero-power setpoint to all connected inverters.
  • ✓ Reset Trip: Restores normal operating state after a trip test.
  • 📉 Set 4.0 kW Limit (ASDU 50): Injects a 4.0 kW active power ceiling command to verify rapid plant curtailment within 5 seconds.
  • Release Curtailment (100 kW): Restores unconstrained plant generation.

13. EV Charging Controls & Belgian Elia Green Sync Boost

GEMS coordinates directly with connected EV chargers to optimize charging currents dynamically:

Charging Mode Current Range Operation & Recommended Application
ECO / Solar Only 6A to 16A / 32A (Dynamic) Charges strictly using solar surplus. If clouds reduce solar, charging automatically throttles or pauses to avoid pulling costly power from the grid. (Note: 6A is the minimum standard EV charging current under IEC 61851: 1.4 kW single-phase or 4.1 kW three-phase).
FAST / Now 16A or 32A (Fixed Max) Draws simultaneously from solar, battery, and utility grid up to the configured circuit breaker phase limit. Use when departing immediately.
Smart Dynamic Dynamic (Spot Driven) Automatically schedules charging during the configured Cheapest Spot Price Hours (1 to 8 hours) of the customer's Day-Ahead contract.

Belgian Elia Green Grid Sync Boost

When enabled under Settings → Strategy, GEMS ingests live 15-minute national renewable energy data from the Belgian transmission operator Elia. When total national green share (wind + solar) exceeds the threshold (e.g. 65%), EV charging and battery pre-charging are automatically boosted, aligning residential consumption with true green energy availability!
